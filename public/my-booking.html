📋 Camp Sandrush Cancellation & Refund Policy

Transparent, fair, and automated refunds computed based on time of cancellation prior to check-in:

Tier 1: Early Notice 100% Refund

Cancellations made more than 7 days before scheduled 3:00 PM check-in qualify for a full 100% refund with zero deduction.

Tier 2: Standard Notice 50% Refund

Cancellations between 3 to 7 days (72h - 168h) before check-in receive a 50% refund, covering partial provisioning costs.

Tier 3: Short Notice 0% Refund

Cancellations within less than 72 hours or no-shows are strictly non-refundable due to reserved camp unit holding.
