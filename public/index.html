Essential Information

Camp Guidelines & FAQs

⏰ Check-in & Check-out Timings

Standard check-in begins at 3:00 PM to allow camp setup and sanitization. Check-out is at 11:00 AM. Late checkout until 3:00 PM is available under Add-ons.

🌊 What is Included in the Stay?

Every stay includes accommodation bedding, welcome coconut drink, evening tea with snacks, buffet breakfast, community campfire with music, and beach sports gear.

🎒 What Should Guests Carry?

Light cotton beach clothing, swimwear, comfortable sandals/flip-flops, sunscreen, sunglasses, personal toiletries, and a government photo ID for check-in verification.

🎸 Weekday vs Weekend vs Events

Weekdays (Mon-Thu) offer peaceful, uncrowded coastlines at attractive rates. Weekends (Fri-Sun) feature vibrant acoustic jams and barbecues. Special Events host full festivals, DJs, and themed culinary spreads!
